What your voice's steadiness can (partly) tell you

Parkinson's disease often shows up in speech first, subtle tremor in pitch and loudness, before it's visible anywhere else. This runs a model trained on the Oxford Parkinson's voice dataset entirely in your browser: nothing you record ever leaves this page.

or upload a .wav / .mp3 file
Hold a single steady vowel sound ("ahhh") at a comfortable pitch and volume for the full clip, that's what the underlying dataset was built from. Background noise or a wavering recording will reduce accuracy.

This is a portfolio / educational demo, not a medical device. It's trained on 195 recordings from 31 people and uses a simplified, browser-based approximation of acoustic features (no clinical-grade signal processing). Cross-validated accuracy is roughly 70%, informative for exploring how voice-based screening works, not for diagnosis. If you have concerns about Parkinson's disease, please talk to a doctor.
